About

Estier designs and builds.

Estier Pty Ltd is a Sydney design, development and construction company. Glass Walls Childcare is its childcare practice: centres designed to the regulations, approved through council and built to a fixed price, by the same people from the first site visit to the licensing inspection.

Design

Estier's design team draws centres for operators and developers and documents them for approval and construction. Where a client brings its own architect, we build to their drawings.

Construction

Licensed builders of commercial and residential work across Sydney. Our commercial fitouts trade as City Fitouts, our homes as Coast Build.

Development

Estier assesses and develops sites of its own, so it reads a childcare site the way a developer does: what it carries, what it costs and what it is worth with a lease on it.

Why glass walls

The practice is named for one idea: a centre where nothing is hidden. Rooms glazed to each other, to the bathrooms and sleep rooms, to reception and to the garden, so educators supervise without moving, parents see the whole centre from the door, and the building is full of light. It is the supervision rule in the National Regulations made visible, and it is what makes a centre photograph well enough to fill its places.
