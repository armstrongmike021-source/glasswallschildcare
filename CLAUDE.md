# Glass Walls Childcare - glasswallschildcare.com

Estier's childcare centre CONSTRUCTION site (Mike, 6 Nov 2026), pitched at architects - first reader Colin Zhang, whose firm has designed 30+ centres. Build-focused: their architect designs, Estier prices the drawings and builds; Estier's own design is mentioned only lightly. Estier IS named (header logo, footer "a practice of
Estier Pty Ltd"), unlike City Fitouts. Domains: glasswallschildcare.com (canonical) and glasswallsdaycare.com (301 here in
`_redirects`); Netlify project `glasswallschildcare` (Estier team, public, glasswallschildcare.netlify.app) deploys from
this repo; both domains moved to Netlify DNS (dns1-4.p09.nsone.net) at GoDaddy on 6 Nov 2026, which ended the old forwarding
to City Fitouts. Static site, no build step: a push to main deploys.

- EVERY page is GENERATED by `tools/build.py` (home, /working-with-architects (replaced /childcare-centre-design-sydney, which 301s to it), /childcare-centre-construction-sydney,
  /examples, /childcare-centre-cost, /childcare-centre-rules-nsw, /childcare-site-check, /about, /contact, thanks.html, gw.css,
  `_redirects`, sitemap.xml, robots.txt). `_redirects` 404s CLAUDE.md, /tools/*, /netlify/* and netlify.toml
  (publish "." serves the repo root): add a line for any new private file. Edit copy in build.py, run `python3 tools/build.py`. Never edit the .html.
- `style.css` is the shared Sydney Fitouts stylesheet; `gw.css` overrides it (navy text, sage-green #2E8B74 accent, sand cards).
  Jost everywhere. No pink.
- Images: eight Grok renders. 6 Nov 2026 (Mike): the old parent-lounge picture was really a cot room, so it is now the sleep-room
  picture (the dark sleep render is retired), and the parent lounge uses corp-light-04-breakout from the same folder from Dropbox/Marketing/Sydney Fitouts (Sep 2026), captioned EVERYWHERE as
  "design concept", never as built work (`RENDERS` in build.py). `estier-logo.png` is the full Estier lockup.
- Wording: Estier "designs" / "design team" - never "architect" or "architecture" for Estier's own service (Sepi is not a
  registered architect, Architects Act 2003); "your architect" for a client's. The build asserts no "Republic", "Trevor",
  "guaranteed". Owners' base price rules do not apply here (no HDA content).
- Calculator (`CALC`/`CALC_JS`): scenario (new / conversion / shell fitout) x places by age (under 2, 2-3, 3-5) x finish
  (standard / premium) + demolition, slope, basement spaces. Rates per m2 of building at 9.5 m2 per licensed place
  (new 4,000-4,800 / 4,800-5,800; conversion 2,600-3,300 / 3,300-4,200; shell 2,000-2,600 / 2,600-3,300), outdoor at 7 m2 a
  place (350-750/m2, podium 500-1,100), site works 150-250k new / 60-120k conversion, basement 75-110k a space, design 7-9%,
  approvals 3-4%. Claude's figures from published guides (Feasly, CBRE) and the City Fitouts childcare rates; Mike to replace
  with real job costs. Also shows the rule minimums: 3.25 m2 indoor, 7 m2 outdoor, parking 1/6 children + 1/2 staff,
  educators at NSW ratios 1:4 / 1:5 / 1:10.
- Rules page sources: Education and Care Services National Regulations Part 4.3 (regs 103-117, 123), SEPP (Transport and
  Infrastructure) 2021 ch 3 + Child Care Planning Guideline 2021 (planning circular PS 21-037), Ku-ring-gai DCP Part 10
  (parking 1/6 + 1/2 staff, 500 m base station, 70 m >33 kV line, 125 m major road, 55 dB(A), refuge 0.25 m2), Central Coast
  DCP 2.10 (18 m width, 40% coverage, 3 m setbacks, 1:20 slope), NCC Class 9b F4D4.
