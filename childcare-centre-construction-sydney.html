Childcare centre construction

Built to a fixed price and the licensing date.

Estier builds childcare centres three ways: new centres on cleared sites, conversions of existing buildings, and fitouts of shells in new developments. The contract is a fixed price off the drawings, and the program is built backwards from the day the NSW Department of Education inspects the centre for its service approval.

A new centre

Sixty to a hundred and twenty places on 1,500 to 4,000 m², usually single storey with the babies' rooms nearest the entry and the preschool rooms opening to the biggest part of the yard. Thirty-six to fifty-two weeks on site after the approval.

  • Demolition, excavation and the slab, with the floor wastes and the hydraulics set out for every bathroom and the kitchen
  • Frame, roof, glazing and the acoustic glass walls
  • Children's bathrooms, nappy change, kitchen, laundry, cot room, staff and office
  • Outdoor play: soft-fall, turf, sandpit, water play, shade sails over at least half the area, fencing a child cannot climb, and the covered verandah
  • Car park, drop-off, fencing, landscaping and the deep-soil planting the council asked for
  • Fire services, access control, CCTV, data and the AV the operator wants

Outdoor play, facing north, with shade over half the area and the verandah as the wet-weather room. Design concept.

A conversion

A house on a 1,000 m² block, a church hall, a medical centre, a shop with a yard, an office floor with a terrace. Converting is a change of use, and a development application, with the same space, parking and outdoor rules as a new centre. What decides it is the structure: most houses only give 3.25 m² a child once two or three walls come out, and most shops need a yard or a podium for the outdoor area. We check the building before you commit, and the program runs twenty to thirty weeks.

A fitout in a new development

Developers are putting centres on the ground floor of apartment and mixed-use buildings, handed over as a shell with the services capped. If the development consent already names a centre-based child care facility, the fitout goes through a private certifier and runs fourteen to twenty-two weeks. The outdoor area is a podium or rooftop: fall-height fencing, soft-fall over the slab, drainage, shade and the acoustic treatment the apartments above will want. We build for the developer before the operator is signed, or for the operator under its lease.
